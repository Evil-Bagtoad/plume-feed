# plume-feed

Daily French reading articles for the Plume Android app's Read tab. The app downloads `feed.json` and nothing else.

- Article texts come from [Vikidia](https://fr.vikidia.org), the encyclopedia for ages 8–13, and are used under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each article in `feed.json` lists its source URL, revision, retrieval date, and what was shortened. Removed passages are marked `[…]`. No words were changed.
- The English translations, glosses, and quiz questions were written for this feed and are also shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
